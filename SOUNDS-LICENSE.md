# Notification sounds

The built-in notification sounds are from Google's
[Material Design sound resources](https://m2.material.io/design/sound/sound-resources.html),
© Google, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They are unmodified, apart from being renamed. The source archive is mirrored at
<https://archive.org/details/material-design-sound-resources>. Both apps credit
them under the "Notification sound" setting.
